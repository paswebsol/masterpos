Business report

Profit calculation

Estimated gross profit = sale total after discount − recorded cost of sold items. Estimated net profit = gross profit − recorded business expenses. Unpaid credit sales are counted as sales on the sale date, so cash received and profit are not the same. Actual results depend on correct buying costs and stock counts.
